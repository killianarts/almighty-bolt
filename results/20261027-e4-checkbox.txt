What it says, and what it does not:
- Every number is in the range I wrote down but one: the reactive library, 14, is one under 15. The
  three conditions I wrote down as making the expectation wrong did not hold: the reactive library
  is smaller than the immediate one (14 against 53), the immediate edit is not larger than the
  retained one's (12 against 22), and no edit is over 26 lines.
- The immediate and reactive edits cost the same, 12 lines each; the retained costs 22, nearly
  twice. The retained app needs a variable for the row and one for the checkbox, =node-visible=,
  and the list written again when the box changes; the other two say (when on ...) where the row
  is. On the choice, on the old base, the edit was 15, 18 and 26 lines (immediate, reactive,
  retained): it came out smaller in each, by 3, 6 and 4, but that is not a comparison, because the
  base is not the same (the apps have been ported to the builder macros since: the retained apps
  are 15 lines longer, record 0026). Telling the checkbox's effect from the port's would need E4
  with the choice made again on this base; it is not done.
- The library costs about as much in the retained (54) and the immediate (53) variants and a
  quarter of it in the reactive, which makes its view and element from the retained node. The
  widgets' tests are larger than the widgets (66, 41 and 67 lines).
- One author wrote the variants, the widgets and the edit. The order of the variants is rotated
  from E4's own, and the first to be done was the cheapest (immediate, 12) tied with the second
  (reactive, 12), so there is no sign here that doing a variant first costs it more.
- The window of each CRUD app is 40 points taller (320), for the checkbox row. The mark in the box
  is a filled square, not a tick.
