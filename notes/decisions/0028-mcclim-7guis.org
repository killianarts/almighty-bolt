#+TITLE: 0028 — 7GUIs apps in McCLIM: the presentation layer and wiring
#+DATE: 2026-10-27
- Status :: proposed

* Context
0027 found McCLIM's gadgets as long and as wired as our retained variant, and
that the stress apps never used the layer that makes
=other-frameworks/task-board.lisp= nice (presentations, translators, typed
commands, accept, updating-output). Micah asked for the 7GUIs apps that have
objects and operations (the crud, the circle drawer, the flight booker) in
McCLIM, to use it. Ours exist in all three variants, so only McCLIM's were
built. =notes/mcclim-7guis.org= was committed with the method, the definition of
wiring, a spec map and what I expected before any app, and says in its results
what was wrong.

* What was done
- The flight booker (an =:accept-values= pane, a date type of the app), the crud
  twice (the layer: presented entries, a translator, an =:accept-values= form;
  and gadgets: a list pane, text fields, buttons, callbacks), and the circle
  drawer (presented circles, a =blank-area= translator, a =:menu= translator,
  =accepting-values= in a window of its own), in
  =apps/7guis/{flight,crud,circles}/mcclim*.lisp=, one system,
  =almighty-bolt-7guis/mcclim=, which =make test= and =make image= never load.
  The models are shared; the circle painter is not (it pushes to our display
  list).
- 40 tests (=make test-mcclim=, 83 assertions) by the acceptance tests of the
  other variants, and =scripts/mcclim-7guis-click.lisp= (=make
  click-mcclim-7guis=), one use of each app on XQuartz with pointer events put
  in McCLIM's queue; =make mcclim-crud=, =mcclim-crud-gadgets=, =mcclim-flight=,
  =mcclim-circles= open them. =scripts/mcclim-size.lisp= takes the 7GUIs paths.

  circles (5 for 3 to 6) was close, the crud's was not (16 or 20 for 4 to 6) and
  the gadgets' was far off (47 for 14 to 18).

* Decision
Proposed: the layer is the immediate way of writing again. Against the retained
and the gadget way it takes the wiring away (two to four times less); against
our immediate variant, which describes the screen from the state each time, it
does not save lines or wiring, and it adds the spec of what the layer cannot do
(6 differently, 4 not met), and what it does for free is validation, a red
field, an option pane, a slider dialog and a menu that greys itself, which in
lines is 8 for the date type and 7 for the dialog against 12 to 25 of ours. So
H2 stays OPEN, and the answer to Micah's question (whether the nice layer takes
the wiring away) is: yes against the gadgets and the retained style, no more
than the immediate variant already does, and at a cost in lines and in gotchas.
(My first count said that the layer had the least wiring in each task; it
counted the variants unevenly, was corrected, and the first count is in the raw
file.)

Proposed, for Micah to choose (the work is on the branch =mcclim=, which has the
stress apps and 0027, not merged and not pushed):
- (a) Merge the branch onto =phase-2= with 0027 and this. =make deps= then
  fetches McCLIM for everyone (80 MB). Merging it and =builder-macros= conflicts
  mildly in the devlog and the progress file. I recommend it, as for 0027.
- (b) Keep it on the branch.
- (c) Apply the change-cost edits E1, E2, E4 and E5 to the McCLIM versions first
  (an Email field in the crud, an optional return date, a hidden filter row, a
  dialog in two columns), which is the test of whether an edit is cheaper
  through the layer. Not done; it can be done on this branch.

* Consequences
- Easier: the comparison of McCLIM and ours now has two kinds of app (stress and
  7GUIs), a rule for wiring and a spec map to hold a size beside. The question
  of whether presentations help can be put to an edit.
- Harder: the =:accept-values= pane of McCLIM master is new (merged 2026) and
  four things in it needed work-arounds; a later McCLIM may change them, and the
  tests call McCLIM's unexported =av-change-query= and =av-execute-action=.
- Open: typing into a field through X (injected keys did not reach the input
  editor); the highlight under a real pointer; the seven other tasks; the models
  as =defclass= (which would drop the =define-presentation-type= lines); E1 to
  E5 on these versions; whether the wiring counts survive another reader.
