#+TITLE: Usability rubric: the sheet, and what was found while writing the edits

The charter's rubric is scored 1 to 5 by the person who wrote the apps first, and later by others.
The apps and the five edits of =notes/change-cost.org= were written by Claude, so the scores are
left for Micah, who reads the code and the patches (=results/change-cost/=) and is the one to say
whether "I could write it without reading the source" is true. What follows the sheet is what
happened while the edits were being written, as facts that can be checked in the patches. They are
not scores.

* What was found
** Size
The seven apps are 576, 547 and 475 lines of code in the retained, reactive and immediate variants,
and use 71, 74 and 53 distinct framework symbols (=results/change-cost/app-sizes.txt=). The immediate
variant is smallest in every task but one (temperature, where retained is smallest) and uses the
fewest symbols in every task.

Those are the sizes at =4cb93b8=, written with the retained and reactive variants' function calls.
Record 0026 (accepted 2026-10-27) merged the builder macros and ported the ten counted apps of those
two variants to them, so the apps you read now have the immediate variant's syntax. After the port
the seven apps are 590, 550 and 475 lines; the ten, 831, 791 and 660 (retained was 816, reactive 791);
the concepts (97, 102 and 73 for the ten) do not change. The patches of E1 to E5 in
=results/change-cost/= are against the apps before the port and do not apply to them now.

** E1, a field in the CRUD form
Each variant had six or seven places to touch: where the field is made, what enables Create, what
picking an entry fills in, Create, Update, Delete, and the layout. The shared model needed an
argument added to two functions, which every caller had to be given: a required argument finds the
callers (it fails when they are run, not when they are compiled).

** E2, an optional return date
One rule ("this text is wrong") was in every variant, written out in each, until the edit moved it
into the model: each variant's change is then one expression, and the cost was in the model. Had the
rule been in the model from the start, the edit would not have touched the variants at all.

** E3, undo
A retained text field does not say what it held before a change, so the retained app keeps a copy of
what both fields held after the last change (=seen-celsius=, =seen-fahrenheit=) and has to enable
and disable the Undo button itself: 31 lines against 18 (reactive, where the old values are still
in the signals) and 12 (immediate, where they are the variables that have not been written yet).

** E4, a group behind a switch
There is no checkbox in any variant; a choice of two options stood in. The retained app needed a
handle on the row to hide it and a call to refresh the list when the switch changes. The immediate
and reactive apps wrap the row in =(when ...)=. Every variant's window had to be taller by the row of
the switch, and the reactive and immediate apps each needed an import that was forgotten and found by a
test. Two things were learned about the variants: the retained =lookup-target= gives the old
rectangle of a control inside a hidden row (the test asks what is drawn instead), and the immediate
variant finds a control by a click outside the window (the CRUD tests passed in it before the window
was made taller, and failed in the other two).

** E5, two columns
The same nine lines in each variant: a row of two columns, a wider window, a shorter one.
